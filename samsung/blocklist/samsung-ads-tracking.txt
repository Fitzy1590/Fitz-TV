# One domain per line - works with Pi-hole, AdGuard Home, NextDNS and most
# router "blocked domains" lists. Blocking a domain also covers its subdomains
# in AdGuard/NextDNS; in Pi-hole add these as wildcard (regex) entries.
#
# If an app or Smart Hub breaks after adding this, remove lines one at a time
# to find the culprit. Core services (samsungcloudsolution.com, time sync,
# app store, firmware updates) are intentionally NOT listed.
